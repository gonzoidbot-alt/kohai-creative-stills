01 / Talking-head videos

Four personas, one script shape.

Each ad: hook (the pain, in their words), then the turn (brief Kōhai, review, approve), then a branded end card. Real lip-synced dialogue, burned-in captions for sound-off viewing, and an “AI-generated persona” label on every frame.

Before launch: these are synthetic people, so Meta’s AI-content disclosure needs to be turned on at upload, and the persona must never be presented as a real customer. Scripts use only verified claims: briefing instead of prompting, Mochi as Chief of Staff, the Library, approval gates, and the 7-day trial. The construction tasks (RFI scope questions, daily logs) are custom-role use cases, so confirm them with product.

The formula — extracted from the winners

1. One sentence, one idea, huge type — the still is a billboard, not a landing page.
2. Two-tone headline: familiar anchor in neutral, the twist in an accent color.
3. Familiar-anchor twist copy: "It's literally ChatGPT…" / "12 AI employees without 12 salaries" — anchor to what they know, subvert it.
4. Characters & scenes over dashboard screenshots — the product is an employee, so it's shown as a character (or pure typography).
5. De-emphasized brand + tiny CTA — the message earns the click.
6. One committed aesthetic per brand, locked template, one variable changed per iteration.
